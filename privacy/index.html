This page covers our website and emails.

This website

happyrose.io has no cookies, analytics, ads, forms or sign-in, and we don't collect anything about you when you visit.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address to keep the service running and secure. See GitHub's privacy statement. Our domain name service is Cloudflare.

Emailing us

Email to happyrose.io addresses is forwarded to our inbox by Cloudflare Email Routing. We use your message and email address only to reply and help you. We don't add you to mailing lists, and we don't sell or share your email. We delete support emails when we no longer need them.

Children

This website isn't directed to children under 13.

If this notice changes, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date.
