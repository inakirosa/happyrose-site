Potato Tracker

Privacy policy

Effective September 29, 2026

The short version: everything you log stays on your phone. We don't collect it, we don't show ads, we don't track you, and we never sell data.

Who we are

Potato Tracker is made by Happy Rose LLC, a Colorado limited liability company ("we", "us"). Questions about privacy: support@happyrose.io.

What the app stores on your phone

The dishes you log, portion sizes, notes, goals, badges, your avatar and your settings are saved only on your device. We can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we collect today

Nothing. Potato Tracker has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It doesn't send your log anywhere.

When you use community features (planned)

We plan to add optional friends, leaderboards and challenges. These will need an account, created with Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google and stored with Google Firebase. If you choose to use them, we'll store your display name, @handle, avatar, potato points totals, earned badges, friends list and challenges. We will never upload your individual food log: what you ate or when stays on your phone.

You'll be able to delete your account and its data inside the app at any time, or by emailing support@happyrose.io. When you delete an account created with Sign in with Apple, we also revoke its Apple sign-in token.

Children

Potato Tracker isn't directed to children under 13, and community features will require you to be 13 or older.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date. We'll tell you in the app before community features start collecting anything.
