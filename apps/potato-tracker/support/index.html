Frequently asked questions

What are potato points?

A simple way to count how much potato you ate. Small portions are 1 potato point, medium 2 and large 3. Your weekly goal is in potato points too.

Is this a diet app?

Nope. It's a fun way to notice what you eat (and maybe discover new potato dishes). There's no calorie counting and no judgement.

Does it need an account?

No. Logging, goals, badges and your avatar work offline with no sign-in. The upcoming community features (friends, leaderboards, challenges) will need an account.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Delete the app and your data goes with it.

How do I delete my data?

Everything you log lives on your phone, so deleting the app deletes it all. When community features arrive, you'll be able to delete your account and its data inside the app, or by emailing support@happyrose.io.

Can I add a dish that's missing?

Yes. Tap Add potato on the Log screen to add more of the 55 built-in dishes, or make your own.

Why is there a map?

Every dish has a home. Potatoes of the world shows where all 55 come from, with a short story for each, and lights up the ones you've eaten. Find it in Settings under "Where do potatoes come from?"
