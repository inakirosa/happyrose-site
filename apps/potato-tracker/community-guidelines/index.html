Community guidelines

Effective September 29, 2026

Friends, leaderboards and challenges are coming to Potato Tracker. They should be fun for everyone, so these rules apply to everything you share: your display name, @handle, avatar and challenges.

Be kind

  • No offensive, hateful, sexual or violent names, handles or content.
  • No harassment, bullying or threats, including through challenges.
  • Don't pretend to be someone else.
  • No spam, ads or links to other services.
  • Never shame anyone about food or their body. This is a potato app, not a judge.

Report and block

On anyone's profile you can Report them if they break these rules, or Block them so they can't see you, add you or challenge you. You can also email support@happyrose.io.

What we do

We review every report within 48 hours. If something breaks the rules we may remove it, reset a name or avatar, suspend the account, or ban it permanently for serious or repeated problems.

Age

You must be 13 or older to use community features.

These guidelines are part of our Terms of Use.
