Potato Tracker

Log every spud.

Baked, fried, mashed or stretched into aligot: Potato Tracker counts every potato you eat on a hand-sized scale. Earn potato points, hit your weekly goal, and collect badges with names like Fry Hard and Tater Tot-al.

How it works

  1. Pick your spud.

    Baked potato? Hash browns? Poutine? Start with 24 favourites and add more whenever you like. There are 55 dishes to discover.

  2. Show us on your hand.

    Tap Small, Medium or Large and watch the potato land in your palm. A small one is a crumb in the palm; a large one swallows the whole hand. Seconds? Tap again.

  3. 1Small2Medium3Large

    Earn potato points.

    Small is 1, medium is 2 and large is 3. Chase your weekly goal and watch the badges roll in.

What's inside

  • The hand scale

    No weighing, no calorie maths. Just "about this much," shown on a hand that matches yours: pick your skin tone, and even your hand size.

  • 9of 14

    Weekly goals

    Set your weekly potato points target (14 to start: two a day), watch the ring fill up, and take on dish goals like "Eat 3 portions of poutine this week."

  • First SpudFry HardMidnight SnackPotato Completionist

    62 badges

    From First Spud to Potato Completionist, with Midnight Snack, Knock Knock, Gnocchi and Poutine on the Ritz along the way. Every unlock gets confetti.

Make your potato.

Your avatar is a potato. Choose the variety (Russet, Yukon Gold, Purple Majesty…), then add eyes, eyebrows, glasses, hair, hats, mustaches and more across 12 categories. Monsieur Spud recommends the beret.

Potatoes of the world.

A map of where 55 potato dishes come from, from Idaho's baked potato to Tanzania's chipsi mayai, each with a short story. Pins go from grey to full colour as you eat your way around the world.

Some potatoes stretch the truth.

Aligot, France's cheesy, stretchy mashed potato, gets its own size scale: the bigger the portion, the further it stretches. Yes, it's wearing a beret.

  • Coming soon

    Friends and challenges

    Add friends, climb the weekly leaderboard, and challenge each other: "Eat 3 portions of gnocchi this week."

  • Private by design

    Your potato log stays on your phone. No account needed to log. When community arrives, it only shares your name, your potato and your potato points totals, never what you ate or when.
