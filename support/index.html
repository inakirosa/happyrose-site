Common questions

How do I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle all purchases and refunds. For the App Store, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play, see Google Play's refund help. We can't see or change your payment.

How do I delete my data?

Each app's privacy policy explains exactly what it stores and how to delete it. For Potato Tracker, everything you log lives on your phone, so deleting the app deletes it. See deleting your data.

I found a bug. What should I send?

Tell us the app, what you did, and what happened. Include your phone model and iOS or Android version. A screenshot helps a lot.
